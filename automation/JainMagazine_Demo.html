Girnar / Sahasavan, Gujarat & Nationwide
Lord Neminath's Janma & Diksha (Tap) Kalyanak Observed
Per the Jain calendar, Shravan Shukla Panchami and Shukla Shashthi mark Neminath's Janma Kalyanak (birth anniversary) and Diksha/Tap Kalyanak (renunciation anniversary) respectively — traditionally observed at Sahasavan near Mount Girnar, his site of renunciation.
